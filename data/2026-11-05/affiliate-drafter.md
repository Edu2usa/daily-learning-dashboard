## Most useful ideas

- Write with economy, clarity, and honest empathy: remove anything that makes the reader work harder, then explain the offer from the reader’s point of view.
- Treat rewriting as a reader check, not cosmetic cleanup: after drafting, ask whether every claim is simple, clear, credible, and easy to act on.
- Make the call to action—the specific next step—immediately visible, supported by short sentences, short paragraphs, and bullets that help readers scan.
- Build trust with specific, supportable details and a recognizably human voice; avoid inflated claims and generic marketing language that could apply to any product.

## Concrete job change

For every affiliate draft, I will add a final reader-first pass that cuts vague hype, checks that each product claim is supportable, and makes one clear next step easy to find.
