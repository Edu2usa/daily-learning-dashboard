## Most useful ideas

- Treat time as genuinely limited rather than trying to fit everything in; choosing what not to do protects attention for the work that matters most.
- Work on a small number of important tasks at once. A fixed limit on unfinished work makes priorities visible and helps important tasks actually reach completion.
- Expect worthwhile work to feel uncomfortable at times. Staying with uncertainty instead of switching to an easier task prevents distraction from becoming avoidance.
- Useful plans should leave room for events and other people that cannot be controlled; flexibility makes execution more reliable than a schedule packed with no margin.

## Concrete job change

For Ed, I will keep a short active-work list, finish or deliberately pause an item before adding another, and leave realistic margin when proposing schedules.
