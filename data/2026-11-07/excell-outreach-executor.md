## Most useful ideas

- Build a focused list of genuine prospects before writing messages; careful selection prevents wasted outreach and makes each pitch more relevant.
- Lead with a customer-focused sales story: describe the problem the prospect may face and the outcome they want, rather than opening with our offer or credentials.
- Use phone, email, voicemail, and social channels as coordinated ways to earn a conversation; each contact should be brief, useful, and consistent instead of repeating the same generic pitch.
- Protect scheduled time for proactive business development and follow-up; steady execution of the basics is more dependable than waiting for referrals or searching for a clever shortcut.

## Concrete job change

For every new affiliate prospect, I will first record one evidence-based reason they fit, then open the outreach with their likely audience need and desired outcome; I will also set the next follow-up date before closing the record.
