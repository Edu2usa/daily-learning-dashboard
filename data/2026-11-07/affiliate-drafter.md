## Most useful ideas

- Make every element earn the next moment of attention: the headline should lead into the opening, and each sentence should give the reader a clear reason to continue.
- Position the offer around the useful result the buyer wants, not a list of product features; then support that result with specific, truthful facts so emotion never outruns evidence.
- Arrange copy like a helpful conversation: anticipate the reader’s questions about fit, price, proof, limitations, and next steps, and answer them in the order they are likely to arise.
- Use only as much copy as the decision requires, then edit away every unnecessary word; unfamiliar, costly, or complex offers need more explanation, while simple choices need less.

## Concrete job change

For each affiliate draft, I will add a short claim-and-proof check before delivery: identify the promised result, place verifiable support beside it, state any important limitation or condition, and remove unsupported urgency or certainty.
